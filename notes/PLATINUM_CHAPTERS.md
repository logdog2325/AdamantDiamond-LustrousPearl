# Platinum's story by chapter (plat-story, 2026-11-07)

The brief for each Platinum chapter agent, in the shape of notes/STORY_CHAPTERS.md (whose rules all hold: notes/STORY_AGENT_BRIEF.md, notes/STORY.md, notes/STORY_HANDOFFS.md). The zone lists are data/platinum/story_chapters.json (`PEARL_VERSION=platinum python3 b2tools/story_chapters.py`); only a Platinum build reads it, and `PEARL_VERSION=platinum python3 b2tools/pearl_story.py chapter <n>` generates a chapter into work/platinum/src. Pearl's and Diamond's data/story_chapters.json is unchanged.

## How to test the whole story (plat-starts, 2026-11-07)
Each chapter starts where the one before it ends. Test them in this order, each from its own start: a start is the chapter before's end state, from Platinum's generated scripts run in story order offline (`PEARL_VERSION=platinum python3 b2tools/platinum_flow_check.py N --end`), and `platinum_flow_check.py N --next` checks it against that end. Each line below goes in front of Platinum's story build, with `test_story_start` listed after `sinnoh_story` (patches/test_story_start.py). The chapter sections are the source: `platinum_flow_check.py --starts` prints these lines from them.
